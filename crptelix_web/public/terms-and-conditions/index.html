Exchange API Connections

Today, the live exchange connection is Binance. Other venues shown in the product are not available yet. When you connect Binance, Cryptelix uses a read-only API key to pull historical balances, trades, and metrics for your workspace.

We do not need trading permissions. We do not need withdrawal permissions. Before a key is stored, we check it with Binance and reject it if reading is off, or if extra permissions are on — including spot and margin trading, futures, options, internal transfer, or withdrawals.

Keys are encrypted at rest and deleted when you remove them. Details of that handling live in our Privacy Policy and Data Security Policy.
